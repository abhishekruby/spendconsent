# Official competition findings

Checked: 13 October 2026. [Rules](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules) govern if other pages differ.

## Required entry

- Build a functioning application with central PayPal sandbox integration and meaningful AI; sponsor tools are optional.
- New work or significant improvements during the submission period.
- Submit a description, public repository with an open-source license and complete run instructions, and public YouTube demo under three minutes.
- A runnable local build is acceptable; static mockups are insufficient. Provide English materials or translations, lawful third-party usage, and original work.
- Keep judge access available through judging. Provide safe setup/access instructions without publishing secrets.

[Official rules, sections 4–6](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules).

## Dates

Deadline: 12 November 2026, noon PST = **13 November 2026, 01:30 IST**. Judging: 1–15 December. Winner timing differs between schedule and rules; reconfirm later. [Schedule](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/details/dates).

## Eligibility

Legal adulthood, geographic restrictions, and conflict-of-interest exclusions apply to every entrant. India is not explicitly excluded, but the user's eligibility is not certified. Devpost registration alone is insufficient. [FAQ](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/details/faqs), [rules section 3](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules).

## Evaluation and prizes

Five equal criteria: implementation, design, impact, innovation, presentation. Grand prizes: $12,000/$8,000/$5,000; five $5,000 honorable mentions; sponsor prizes have tool-specific conditions. At most one grand-or-honorable plus one sponsor prize. [Rules sections 6 and 8](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules).

## Resources reviewed

The [official resource hub](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/resources) links Checkout, REST APIs, JavaScript SDK v6, sandbox setup, Invoicing, Subscriptions, Payouts, Disputes, Donations, Webhooks, and AI tools. Sponsor tools include AG Grid, APIMatic, Astropods, Bryntum, Channel3, Elastic, KERNEL, Postman, Render, and Zapier. Their costs and quotas have not been verified; none has been adopted.

[PayPal AI Toolkit](https://github.com/paypal/AI-Toolkit) offers coding-agent skills and sandbox MCP integration. This supports development but does not by itself supply the product's AI intelligence. No toolkit installation or access-token configuration was performed.

## Our planning checklist

These are project practices, not extra competition rules:

- Validate eligibility before submission and recheck amendments near the deadline.
- Confirm license choice with the user; do not publish private configuration.
- Plan a demo showing the actual PayPal and AI flow and a recoverable failure.
- Obtain permission before publishing the repository, video, or final entry.
