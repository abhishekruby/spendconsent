Supply comparison tasks

For each request, find the offer with the lowest full delivered total that meets every requirement, or explain why none qualifies.

  • The exact number of items is required: no extra items, no mixing products.
  • Full total = pack price × packs + tax + delivery. Tax is 10% of the item subtotal (not delivery), rounded to the cent. Delivery is charged once per order.
  • Write down: product code (SKU), packs, total items, subtotal, tax, delivery, full total, or “no match” and why.
  • Say “final” when you are done. All prices are fictional, in USD, and for one-time purchases.

Practice (untimed) Exactly 10 spray bottles, 500 ml each, for at most USD $25.00 including tax and delivery. One-time purchase only.
